# About

## Why this exists

Every time someone stands up data & AI governance in a new organisation, the
first few months look the same: draft the framework, draft the policies, build
the RACI, stand up the committee, build the registers, write the board pack.
Months of work — and almost none of it is secret. It's the same scaffolding
every organisation needs.

This kit gives that scaffolding away. It's a **genuinely open resource** —
CC BY 4.0, no accounts, no paywall, no email capture. Take it, adapt it, and
spend your months on the parts that are actually specific to your organisation.

## What it is — and isn't

It **is** a set of practical starting templates covering data and AI governance
end to end, including EU AI Act readiness.

It **is not** legal advice, a compliance guarantee, or a substitute for
professional judgement. Governance is contextual: your jurisdiction, sector,
size, and risk appetite all change what "good" looks like. Use these as a head
start, then make them yours and have the material bits reviewed by qualified
counsel.

## How it's built

A static site generated with
[MkDocs Material](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/) and hosted free
on GitHub Pages. The whole thing is Markdown in a public repo — which means
anyone can read the source, suggest changes, or fork their own version.
