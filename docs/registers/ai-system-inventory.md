# AI System Inventory

<span class="pill ready">Ready</span>

**Purpose.** The central record of every AI system: purpose, risk tier, data, model/vendor, owner, and lifecycle stage. The foundation of AI Act compliance.

**When to use it.** Populate before anything else; keep current.

**How to use it.** Copy the structure below, replace the bracketed placeholders,
and ratify it through your governance committee. Keep it proportionate to your
size and risk.

---

## The template

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| System ID | AI-001 |
| Name & purpose | [what it does] |
| Provider / model | [built / vendor + model] |
| Role | Provider / Deployer |
| Risk tier | Prohibited / High / Limited / Minimal |
| Data used | [sources, personal data Y/N] |
| Accountable owner | [role] |
| Lifecycle stage | Ideation / Dev / Live / Retired |
| EU AI Act status | [assessed? conformity route?] |

---

## Adaptation notes

- **Small organisations:** keep the fields but reduce the cadence and merge roles.
- **Regulated sectors:** align terminology and controls to your supervisory
  expectations and to the [EU AI Act readiness checklist](../eu-ai-act/readiness-checklist.md).
- **Non-EU:** swap regulatory references for your local regime; the structure holds.
